# Three Metrics - Verification Report

## ✅ The app NOW correctly represents the difference between:

### 1. **Recognition** (Q02)
- **Definition**: "Have you seen this element before?"
- **Calculation**: % who said "Yes, definitely" OR "Yes, I think so"
- **Example**: Electric Green = 37.6% (378 out of 1,005 respondents)
- **What it measures**: Exposure and familiarity

### 2. **Attribution** (Q05)
- **Definition**: "Which brand does this belong to?"
- **Calculation**: % of ALL respondents who said "Škoda"
- **Example**: Electric Green = 17.4% (175 out of 1,005 respondents)
- **What it measures**: Brand identification
- **Includes**: Don't Know responses in denominator

### 3. **Uniqueness** (Q05)
- **Definition**: Same question, different calculation
- **Calculation**: Škoda ÷ (Škoda + Other brands), **excluding "Don't Know"**
- **Example**: Electric Green = 44.4% (175 ÷ [175 + 219])
- **What it measures**: Distinctive ownership vs competitors
- **Shows**: Among people who gave a brand answer, % who said Škoda

## Key Relationships

1. **Recognition ≥ Attribution** (always true)
   - You can SEE something without knowing whose it is
   - Electric Green: 37.6% ≥ 17.4% ✅

2. **Uniqueness ≥ Attribution** (always true)
   - Excluding Don't Know makes the denominator smaller
   - Electric Green: 44.4% ≥ 17.4% ✅

3. **Attribution Gap** = Recognition - Attribution
   - Electric Green: 37.6% - 17.4% = **20.2%**
   - Meaning: 20.2% have SEEN it but don't know it's Škoda's
