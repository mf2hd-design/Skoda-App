### 4. Owner vs Non-Owner Recognition

**Report Finding (Slide 17-18):**
> "Among Škoda owners the average recognition and attribution increases"
> "Among Non-Owners, Škoda's recognition drops 20%pts and attribution 8%pts"

**App Data (Recognition after 6 elements):**
- **Škoda owners:** 60.0%
- **Non-Škoda owners:** 47.0%
- **No car:** 29.5%

**Difference:** 60% - 47% = 13 percentage points

⚠️ **Status:** SLIGHT VARIATION
- Report says "20%pts drop" in recognition
- App shows 13pp difference
- **Likely explanation:** Report may be comparing recognition (Q02 "seen before") vs app shows attribution (QHiddenAwareness "identified Škoda")
- Both tell same story: owners recognize better
