## Key Findings

**High-Recognition Segments (+6-7% lift):**

• **Learning-Oriented** (47% of audience): 45.6% recognition vs 38.7% non-learners
  - +6.9% lift – highest brand awareness
  - Recognize fastest (4.71 elements vs 4.83)
  - **Strategy:** Innovation, technology, progress messaging

• **Traditional Values** (41% of audience): 45.9% recognition vs 39.3% non-traditional
  - +6.6% lift despite heritage focus
  - Need fewest elements (4.67) to recognize
  - **Strategy:** Heritage, reliability, established trust

• **Adventure-Seeking - Challenges** (40% of audience): 45.8% recognition vs 39.4%
  - +6.4% lift with novelty-seekers
  - **Strategy:** Bold, exciting, exploratory messaging

**Underperforming Segment:**

• **Strong Sense of Adventure** (38% of audience): 40.9% vs 42.6% = **-1.7% lift**
  - Only segment that underperforms vs non-segment
  - Requires most elements (4.82) to recognize
  - **Insight:** Self-identified "adventurers" may need experiential/sensory approach, not just cognitive

## Market-Specific Patterns

- **Spain:** Highest on adventure-seeking (45%) – align with Sonic's energetic personality
- **Poland:** Highest on tradition (46%) + learning (47%) – heritage + progress narrative works
- **Germany:** Most pragmatic (lowest on both extremes) – rational benefits messaging
- **UK:** Balanced profile (strongest adventure sense at 43%) – multi-faceted approach

## Strategic Application

**Media Planning:**
- Over-index on learning-oriented and traditional value audiences (6-7% recognition lift)
- Target channels/content that attracts these segments

**Creative Strategy:**
- Dual messaging approach: Innovation + Heritage reaches 87% of high-recognition audiences
- Learning segment → Technology, innovation, progress
- Traditional segment → Symbol, heritage, reliability
- Challenge-seekers → Novelty, excitement, bold choices

**Budget Allocation:**
- Prioritize segments with +6-7% lift over those with neutral/negative lift
- Test experiential/sensory creative for "strong adventure" segment anomaly
